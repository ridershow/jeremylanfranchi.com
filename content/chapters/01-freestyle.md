2007. A scooter in Rueil-Malmaison, and a sport I had to invent a relationship with.

I was not waiting for a syllabus. Freestyle scootering was hours outside, a scene that rewarded curiosity as much as commitment, and the stubborn pleasure of trying again. I stayed open. I also stayed in it.
